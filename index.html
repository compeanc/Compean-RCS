Services

Specialized ABA revenue cycle consulting, with hands-on support where it matters most.

Revenue Cycle Audits & Assessments

Independent reviews of claims, payment trends, and billing performance to identify revenue gaps, recurring errors, and areas for improvement.

Denial Management & Appeals

Direct claim-level investigation and resolution, including payer follow-up, corrections, reconsiderations, and appeals. More than recommendations—I work the denials.

A/R Management & Revenue Recovery

Focused follow-up on aging and unpaid claims, prioritizing balances by payer trends, outstanding dollars, and recovery opportunities.

RCM Analysis & Workflow Optimization

Collection-rate and denial-trend analysis, process reviews, and practical workflow recommendations to address root causes and reduce repeat issues.

WHO YOU'LL WORK WITH:

Lily Compean

Founder & Principal Consultant

With over 10 years of outpatient therapy revenue cycle experience, I founded Compean Revenue Cycle Solutions to help ABA practices understand where revenue gets stuck—and take action to address it. I combine independent billing reviews, payer and collection-trend analysis, and practical workflow consulting with hands-on claim work: investigating denials, submitting appeals, and following up on unpaid balances. Whether your practice needs a focused audit, a denial recovery project, or help improving its billing processes, you work directly with me to uncover issues and build a more effective revenue cycle.
