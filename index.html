Services

Comprehensive RCM tailored for ABA, PT, OT, and ST clinics.

Eligibility & Verifications

Stop denials before the patient even walks through the door. Rigorous, proactive verification of benefits ensures absolute clarity on coverage, copays, and deductibles.

Prior Authorizations

Avoid gaps in care and lost revenue. Proactive tracking of expiring approvals and swift handling of new authorizations means your therapists never miss a session.

Claims Submission

Clean claims, submitted fast. Specialized expertise in intricate CPT requirements and dual-service coding rules drastically reduces initial rejection rates.

Payment & ERA Posting

Meticulous cash application. Fast, accurate posting of Electronic Remittance Advices and patient payments keeps your ledger transparent and up to date.

A/R Management

Don't let aging claims sit. Aggressive follow-up identifies workflow bottlenecks, appeals unjust denials, and recovers the revenue your practice has already earned.

Denials & Appeals

Aggressive denial management. Every rejected claim is systematically investigated, corrected, and appealed to capture every dollar you are owed.

Statement & Collections

Professional, transparent patient invoicing. Tactful, clear management of patient balances improves collection rates while maintaining positive patient relationships.

WHO YOU'LL WORK WITH:

Lily Compean

Founder & Principal Consultant

With over 10 years of experience navigating the complexities of healthcare operations, I founded Compen Revenue Cycle Solutions to bring enterprise-level billing expertise directly to individual ABA providers and growing clinics. My background is deeply hands-on: I have managed high-volume revenue cycles and executed intricate EMR workflows for large-scale organizations. Whether you are a solo practitioner needing full-cycle billing management, or a larger clinic requiring overflow support during peak times, I step in directly to ensure you get paid. Beyond daily billing, I also provide strategic RCM workflow consultation and actively advise on EMR software development, utilizing real-world operational insight to help optimize your practice from the ground up.
