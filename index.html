Services

Specialized ABA revenue cycle consulting, with hands-on support where it matters most.

Revenue Cycle Audits & Assessments

Independent reviews of claims, payment trends, and billing performance to identify revenue gaps, recurring errors, and areas for improvement.

Denial Management & Appeals

Hands-on investigation and resolution of denied claims, including payer follow-up, claim corrections, reconsiderations, and appeals.

A/R Management & Revenue Recovery

Focused follow-up on aging and unpaid claims, prioritizing balances by payer trends, outstanding dollars, and recovery opportunities.

RCM Analysis & Workflow Optimization

Collection-rate and denial-trend analysis, process reviews, and practical workflow recommendations to address root causes and reduce repeat issues.

About the Founder

Lily Compean

Founder & Principal Consultant

With over 10 years of experience in outpatient therapy revenue cycle management, my background spans front-office operations through revenue cycle leadership. This has given me a thorough understanding of how billing processes connect and how issues can affect a practice's financial performance.

I founded Compean Revenue Cycle Solutions to provide independent and growing ABA practices with experienced, personalized support. My approach combines detailed analysis with hands-on problem-solving to help practices address billing challenges and improve their operations.
